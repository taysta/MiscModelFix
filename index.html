The misc_model_static fix

Jedi Academy culls each misc_model_static by checking whether one point, its origin plus zoffset, is in the viewer's PVS. Models mounted on walls or hanging from ceilings often have that point inside the wall or in the void, where it has no PVS cluster, so the game never draws them. The fix picks a point that is actually in open space (the centre of the model's bounds, then its corners) when the models are loaded. Models that are completely buried stay hidden.

Collision: these models have no collision of their own, so it only comes from map brushes. Each model's mesh vertices were checked against the map's solid and clip brushes: 60% or more inside means collision, 20 to 59% partial, under 20% none. "Out of reach" means the lowest visible part is more than 128 units above the floor (not shown on the yavin maps, whose ground is a mesh).
